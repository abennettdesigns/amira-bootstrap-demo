How To Play Scales By Memory Fast!

Slow & Steady Wins The Race

Start slower than performance tempo. Keep fingers close to the fingerboard.

Train Accuracy
Give Me Some Rhythm's, Baby!

Instead of straight 16ths, try a long-short, short-long and other variations of rhythms.

Strengthen Fingers
Use "Burst" Speed Practice

Play 4 notes FAST. Stop. Reset. Repeat with the next 4 notes.

Build Speed
Hot Hands

Pretend the fingerboard is hot lava; place the finger down quickly then release.

Build Speed
Be A Master of the Bow

Fast scales need: smooth string crossings, fluid bow speed and even contact point.

Scale Mapping

Learn to see every accidental, pattern ad interval so the fingerboard becomes a map, not a mystery.
